# Nurture tracks

## Tracks
| Track | Content | For |
|---|---|---|
| Line A | Application notes, protocols, media guidance, webinar invitations | Contacts with line A or tissue research |
| Line B | Core area 2 protocols, application data, comparisons | Blood and immune research |
| Compound Sets | Product highlights, bundle offers, protocol tips | Any research group using differentiation or culture reagents |

Each track is a short series of emails over 12-16 weeks, one useful item per send, never a sales pitch first.

## Entry rules
A contact enters only if all are true:
- Fit score passes the nurture threshold (good fit, not ready).
- Consent is confirmed (opt-in, or lawful basis; extra check for EU, UK and Swiss contacts).
- Not suppressed: competitor, customer account, open deal, small-company exclusion.
- Product interest is known or inferred from research area.

## Engagement points
Click 5 · pricing, product or protocol page visit 8 · webinar attendance 10. Opens are not counted, because security scanners inflate them.

## Promotion to a rep
- **Immediately** on any hand-raise: reply, quote or sample request, pricing-page visit, meeting booked.
- **Otherwise** when engagement points reach 30.
- The workflow removes the contact from nurture and creates a rep task with context.

## Exit rules
- No engagement after ten weeks: move out of nurture to hold, and stop marketing email.
- Unsubscribe: email off immediately.
- Bounce: remove and review the address.

## Synthetic results (16 weeks)
| Track | Contacts | Promoted to a rep |
|---|---|---|
| Line A | 2,400 | 3.5% |
| Line B | 1,800 | 3.7% |
| Compound Sets | 3,000 | 3.2% |

## Monitoring
Entry volume, promotion rate by track, time to promotion, share exiting without engagement, unsubscribe rate, and conversion of promoted contacts to meetings.
