# ICP cards

All figures come from the synthetic won-deal set.

## Card 1: Academic and research-hospital labs (lab buyer)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | University labs, research hospitals, government labs |
| Win rate | About 37% of deals; 169 deals in the sample |
| Typical won deal | About $2.3K |
| Why they buy | Reliable, ready-to-use cells and reagents for experiments and grant-funded projects |
| Buyer personas | PI or professor (decides), scientist or lab manager (evaluates and orders), postdoc (influences), procurement (processes the order) |
| Best first product | Cell Line B or a starter small-molecule set |
| Triggers | New grant award, new paper on the topic, new lab setup, new PI |
| Disqualifiers | Student-only contact, competitor-sponsored lab |
| Message | Reproducible results, protocol support, fast delivery |

## Card 2: Organ-on-chip and platform developers (programme buyer)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | Companies building vascularised and organ-on-chip models |
| Win rate | About 32%; 50 deals |
| Typical won deal | About $9.2K |
| Why they buy | Need consistent human cell sources and media to build barrier and perfusion models |
| Buyer personas | Head of R&D or biology (decides), scientist (evaluates), founder or CSO (sponsors), procurement |
| Best first product | Cell Line A with media |
| Triggers | Funding round, new platform launch, hiring in vascular or in vitro toxicology, conference presence |
| Disqualifiers | Competitor already embedded with an exclusive agreement, company under 11 people |
| Message | Application data, scalability, technical support |

## Card 3: CROs and CDMOs (service buyer)
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Who | Contract research and manufacturing organisations offering in vitro services |
| Win rate | About 28%; 39 deals |
| Typical won deal | About $3.1K |
| Why they buy | Standardised cells for client studies |
| Buyer personas | Founder or executive, scientific director, project lead, procurement |
| Best first product | Cell Line A |
| Triggers | New service launch, new client programme, expansion |
| Disqualifiers | Resells to competitors only |
| Message | Lot consistency, documentation, supply security |
