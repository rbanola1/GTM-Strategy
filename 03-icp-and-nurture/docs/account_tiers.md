# Account tiers

## Method
Add points for segment, role and organisation size (fit points from the win-rate analysis), then set tiers from the totals and check that win rate and deal size separate cleanly.

| Tier | Meaning | Synthetic result | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Best-fit accounts and roles | 71% win rate | Named rep, personal outreach, signal monitoring |
| **B** | Good fit | 36% win rate | Signal-based sequence, rep reviews before sending |
| **C** | Possible fit, lower confidence | 20% win rate | Nurture track, promote on a hand-raise |
| **Excluded** | Competitors, tiny companies, students, existing customers | - | Suppressed from outreach |

## Rules
- One decision-maker plus at most one escalation contact per account.
- Tier is an account property; individual contacts are scored separately.
- Large strategic programme accounts can be placed in a priority list regardless of the formula, because deal value is not captured by contact scoring.
- Re-tier every quarter from new won and lost deals.
