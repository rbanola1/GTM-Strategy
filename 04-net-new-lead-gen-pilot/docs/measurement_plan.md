# Measurement plan

## Comparison
New process (this pilot) versus existing process (keyword pulls, bulk e-blast, reps follow opens).

| Metric | How measured | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh contact share | Contacts not already in the CRM / contacts delivered | Above 90% |
| Email find rate | Emails found / people searched | Above 60% |
| Bounce rate | Bounced / sent | Under 2% |
| Positive reply rate | Positive replies / delivered (exclude auto-replies, out-of-office, unsubscribe) | 1% or more; aim 2-4% |
| Wrong-person rate | Wrong person or wrong field replies / real replies | Under 10% |
| Meetings | Completed meetings per source | Set after month one |
| Cost per lead, per positive reply, per meeting | Credits and tool cost / outcome | Falling month on month |

## Forecast
Run a Monte Carlo before sending: sample each tier's reply rate from a distribution, simulate the list, report the mean and the 10th-90th percentile. Past data: targeted sends convert roughly ten times better than bulk.

## Decision at week six
Buy or expand the tool only if all are true: targeted reply rate is at least 1%; the limit on volume is data (find rate below 60%, bounce above 2%, or industry roles missing); and reps have capacity to follow up.
