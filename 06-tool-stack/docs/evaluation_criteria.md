# Evaluation criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Question | Score 5 means |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solves a measured bottleneck | 30 | Which step of the funnel is limiting results, and does this tool fix it? | Directly removes the current limit |
| Data quality | 20 | How accurate, fresh and complete is the data it produces? | Verified, high find rate, low bounce |
| CRM integration | 15 | Does it connect cleanly and respect the data-ownership rules? | Native, one-way, mapped fields |
| Cost | 15 | Total monthly cost including credits and seats | Free or inside existing spend |
| Effort to run | 10 | How much weekly work does it need? | Almost none |
| Risk | 10 | Lock-in, compliance, deliverability, vendor stability | Low |

## Scoring
Weighted score = sum of (weight x score) divided by the maximum, shown out of 100. Compare tools within the same funnel stage; do not buy two for one job.

## Checks before any purchase
- Is the bottleneck proven with numbers (find rate, bounce rate, reply rate, rep capacity)?
- Can an existing tool do 80% of the job?
- What is the exit plan, and can data be exported?
- Who owns the tool, and who is allowed to write into the CRM from it?
- What is the real unit cost after a small test?
