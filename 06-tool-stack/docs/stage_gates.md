# Stage gates

| Stage | Spend per month | Adds | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Prove | about $40 | Existing tools; website and company intent data | Process, ICP, routing, baseline KPIs |
| 2 Scale data | about $225 | Enrichment platform, pay-as-you-go verification, extra CRM credits if needed | About twice the targeted volume, industry contacts, job-change and hiring signals |
| 3 Target system | about $520 | Social selling tool for two reps, scheduled automation, enrichment on a higher plan with native CRM sync | Lists, enrichment and signals refresh automatically; reps spend time on conversations |

## Gates
**Stage 1 to 2** needs all three:
1. Targeted reply rate at least 1% (the process works).
2. The limit is data: find rate under 60%, or bounce over 2%, or industry roles missing.
3. Reps have capacity to follow up more leads.

**Stage 2 to 3** needs all of:
1. Pipeline growing per lane.
2. Credits fully used.
3. Spend above the new-tool cap approved by leadership.

## Not recommended (and what would change that)
| Tool | Why not now | Revisit if |
|---|---|---|
| Dedicated cold-email stack with new domains | CRM sequences cover current volume | Volume above about 1,000 sends per day |
| AI sales development agent | The problem is who and when, not sending capacity | Targeting works and reps are full |
| Pharma intelligence feed | Few pharma targets today | Tier A pharma list grows past about 30 accounts |

## Review
Re-score tools every quarter and after each stage; retire anything unused.
