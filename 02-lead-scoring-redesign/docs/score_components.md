# Score components (0-100)

| Component | Max | What it measures | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company fit | 30 | Is the organisation a real buyer | Segment, size band, past buyer |
| Contact fit | 25 | Is this person the right buyer | Role, research area relevance |
| Engagement | 25 | Interest shown by the contact | Email clicks, replies, form fills, repeat visits |
| Intent | 20 | Is now the time | Pricing visit, quote request, new grant, new paper |

## Changes from the old engagement-only score
| Old rule | Problem | New rule |
|---|---|---|
| Opens score points | Inflated by scanners | Opens score nothing |
| Any form scores the same | Newsletter equals quote request | Quote and sample forms are intent; newsletter forms score little |
| Any page visit scores the same | Blog equals pricing | Pricing and product pages are intent; blog scores little |
| Sequence enrolment and our calls score points | Measures our action | Removed |
| No fit | Cannot separate buyers from bystanders | Company and contact fit added |

## Hard rules
Competitor domain, below minimum company size, student-only contact: score is zero.
