# Measurement

## Definitions
| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Delivered | Sent minus bounced |
| Real reply | A human reply; excludes auto-replies, out-of-office, delivery failures and unsubscribes |
| Positive reply | A real reply that expresses interest, asks a question, requests a sample or agrees to a meeting |
| Wrong-person reply | A real reply that says the recipient is not the right person; track separately |
| Meeting | Completed meeting booked from the sequence |
| Click | A recipient clicked a link; more reliable than opens |

## Why not opens
Security scanners open and click links automatically, so open rates are inflated and unreliable. Use replies and meetings.

## KPIs
| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Bounce rate | Under 2% |
| Real reply rate | At least 1%; aim 2-4% for targeted lists |
| Wrong-person rate | Under 10% of real replies |
| Sends per real reply | Falling |
| Meetings per 100 sends | Rising |

## Reporting
One weekly view by list: sent, delivered, real replies, positive replies, meetings, samples, deals. Pause any list that falls under the stop rule.
